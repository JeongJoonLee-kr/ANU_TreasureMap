### Meeting Minutes

**1. Brainstorming Session: Defining "What is Treasure"**

The team discussed the core concept of "treasure." Below is a summary of each member's ideas:

- **Shaoming:**

  - Proposed the focus should be on dynamic events and user experiences (e.g., collection books), not on finding buildings, as ANU maps already exist.
  - Suggested that users should be allowed to update event information.
  - Recommended the platform could be an app or a simple game.
  - Identified platform payment fees as a potential risk.

- **Joon:**

  - Suggested a webpage with an interactive campus map where users can add pins for events or social places (lunch, chill spots) with tags.
  - Envisioned pins having pop-up images and information.
  - Proposed a game element: an image-recognition quiz ("Do you know where this is?").
  - Voiced a risk: users might lose interest after they have found all the initial treasures.

- **Xiyin:**

  - Recommended adding rating and comment features for events to differentiate the platform from Google Maps.
  - Proposed an achievement system where users can upload images to complete goals.
  - Suggested user "roles" (e.g., "sport," "art") that would filter the map to show treasures based on their preferences.

- **Zijun:**

  - Expanded on the achievement system idea (e.g., achieve "Food King" status).
  - Suggested using different colored pins to categorize treasures (e.g., working spaces, club locations, food vendors).
  - Proposed a forum feature integrated with the map, where a discussion thread could be a pin and could include links to club webpages.
  - Identified the need for content filtering as a risk to manage inappropriate user uploads.

- **Wing:**

  - Proposed hiding keywords or using "question mark" pins on the map to encourage exploration, perhaps with an on/off toggle.
  - Suggested a pop-up notification feature to alert users when they are near a treasure.
  - Pointed out the challenge of deciding what information to hide versus what to show.

- **Suya:**

  - Proposed creating "Treasure Hunt Missions" with specific tasks (e.g., "find and photograph a black swan").
  - Suggested a "Pokémon GO" style collecting game where users build a "Nature Dex" by photographing campus animals and plants.
  - Envisioned specific locations as "eco gyms" for biodiversity quizzes or team challenges.
  - Recommended real-time notifications for random events to encourage spontaneous exploration.

- **Anlin:**
  - Proposed running a continuous, themed activity each semester (e.g., finding all campus animals), with a winner at the end.
  - Suggested that winners could create puzzles for the next round of participants.
  - Recommended connecting treasure hunt events with real-world festivals.
  - Identified the risk of sustaining winner prizes, suggesting collaboration with the university or clubs as a solution.

**2. Working Agreement**

The team formally established its initial working agreement to ensure smooth collaboration.

- **Core Working Hours:** Team members will aim to be available between **10:00 AM and 4:00 PM**.
- **Communication Channel:** **Microsoft Teams** is the designated primary channel for all team communication.
- **Sickness Protocol:**
  - **Slight Symptoms:** The member should inform the team and plan to work remotely.
  - **Serious Symptoms:** The member should inform the team. The team will redistribute their work, and the member can catch up after recovery.
- **Pull Request (PR) Rules:** The team agreed that a formal set of rules for PRs is needed but deferred the creation of these rules to a later time.
