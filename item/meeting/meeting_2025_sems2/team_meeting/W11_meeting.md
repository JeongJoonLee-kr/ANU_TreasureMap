## Meeting Minutes
- **Minecraft Team (JeongJoonLee's Group) Update:**
    - The team will be building the game's environment in Minecraft.
    - Their initial strategy is to first establish the overall map area and then divide it into distinct zones for focused construction.
    - A key next step is to prepare a time estimate for the building phase.

- **Game Engine Team (Suya's Group) Update:**
    - The team has defined the main narrative journey: The Detective Bear receives a message from a "phantom thief" and takes on the case, seeing it as a chance to become famous.
    - The first explorable location in the game will be **Kambri**.
    - The narrative will feature a main treasure and several side treasures.
    - A side quest concept was proposed: "The Delicious Coffee," which starts by investigating a leftover coffee at night. This is currently marked as **To Be Decided (TBD)**.

- **Next Steps & Planning:**
    - The Game Engine Team members will each brainstorm one treasure idea for the Kambri location.
    - All ideas will be discussed next Wednesday to decide on the main and side treasures.
    - Team members will also research "daily-life mystery" stories for narrative inspiration.
    - Next week's goals are to: 1) Plan the entire game flow. 2) Design the game's cognitive load to ensure it is accessible. 3) Prepare for the concept testing
