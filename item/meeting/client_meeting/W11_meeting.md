## Meeting Minutes
- **Team A – Godot “detective teddy” concept**
  - Story-driven, ANU at night (mystery tone), branch stories per map spot; **dice-based active skills** + **passive checks**; “rules” the player discovers; **detective office** becomes a personal exhibit of collected treasures.
  - **Client guidance:** Minimise *horror*; steer to **celebration/feel-good ANU** (mystery OK). Consider **day/night** option later; version-1 should be uplifting. Keep **onboarding light** (avoid heavy rulebooks). 
  - **Design risks:** Content pacing/volume, permission/privacy when depicting clubs/people, randomness frustration—add **fail-safes**, consider **difficulty modes** and **weighted dice**.

- **Team B – Minecraft exploration mode**
  - Scaled campus interior/exterior to **enable virtual exploration**, hidden treasures, simple quests/quiz, **achievements**; demoed **HIVE** interior build with treasures. 
  - **Use case:** newcomers/current students explore places **Google/Street View can’t show** (inside buildings, niche spaces). Mode is **open-ended (“wandering”)**; optional NPC hints. 
  - **Constraints/risks:** Time-intensive worldbuilding, platform constraints inside Minecraft, content pipeline for quests/events. Popularity vs. alternatives noted; still acceptable to leverage a known platform. 

- **Cross-team observations**
  - Potential **convergence:** reuse Minecraft captures (video/assets) to inspire/seed Godot content; align visual style (pixel/low-fi). 
  - **Next-week expectation:** each team returns with **evidence from quick concept tests** (5–10 peers/staff) and **scoping estimates** (what slice of campus or narrative can be built this term). Share current decks in Teams for version history.
