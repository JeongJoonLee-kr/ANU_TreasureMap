## Meeting Minutes

**1. Sharing Individual Ideas for Treasures & Features**

- **Shaoming:**
    - Suggested including a **collection/achievement book** as part of the treasure hunt.
    - Proposed **QR code scanning or geo-location checking** as validation for completing tasks.
    - Emphasized changing the scene to reflect **seasonal or campus vibes** (e.g., graduation, orientation).

- **Zijun:**
    - Proposed an **interactive map** where users can leave comments at hidden or interesting spots.
    - Suggested a **“dictionary” of allowed words** to make comments more structured, creating a mystery element for discovery.
    - Envisioned **shortcuts or hidden places** being revealed through these comments.

- **Joon:**
    - Focused on **UI ideas**: colourful map, pins for different categories (restaurants, lectures, toilets).
    - Suggested a **quiz-like game** using campus photos where users guess the location.
    - Highlighted difference between **abstract game ideas** (experience, interaction) vs. **concrete solutions** (UI features).

- **Xiyin:**
    - Proposed a **3D treasure city** concept where treasures can be shared as photos or videos.
    - Suggested **gift exchange** (users leave a digital gift at a location for others to discover).
    - Highlighted the **achievement system** and “Easter egg” type treasures.

- **Suya:**
    - Suggested enabling users to **create their own treasures**, similar to *Stardew Valley* building.
    - Proposed **interactive treasures** like plants or pets (users can feed/water them).
    - Recommended **sharing personal “islands”** where friends can visit each other’s collections.

- **Wing:**
    - Focused on **exploration and puzzles**—treasures should require step-by-step discovery.
    - Proposed **temporary treasures** (only available for a limited time).
    - Suggested **surprise notifications/pop-ups** to spark curiosity and urgency.

- **Anlin:**
    - Proposed the team work, like three persons group:
        1. **Brain challenges** (math/coding puzzles).
        2. **Sports challenges** (running, table tennis).
        3. **Lucky boxes** (random rewards/penalties).
    - Suggested **festival-themed long-term treasures** (Halloween, Mid-Autumn Festival).
    - Introduced a **storyboard system** where treasures form personalized stories.
    - Recommended **basic structures (like ANU Sports)** that can be decorated with unlocked items.

**2. Consolidation of Ideas**
- Group identified overlapping themes:
    - **Collection & achievements** (Shaoming, Xiyin, Anlin).
    - **Interactive/social features** (Zijun, Suya, Wing).
    - **Seasonal/festival themes** (Shaoming, Anlin).
    - **Exploration & surprise** (Wing, Joon).
