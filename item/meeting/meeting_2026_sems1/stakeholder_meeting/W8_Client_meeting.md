**Final Deliverable Scope**
*   **Vertical Slice:** The team will deliver a "Proof of Concept" or "Pilot" version, featuring the onboarding process and the first week of gameplay.
*   **Concept Presentation:** Ideas that could not be implemented due to time—such as potential features and future storylines—will be documented in a **presentation deck** to demonstrate the project's full depth.
*   **Future Handover:** This documentation will serve as a guide for the next team, detailing where to start and how the "ideal" version of the game should look.

**Refining Gameplay & UX**
*   **Narrative Resources:** The game uses a system of **"$50 and 7 days (Time)"** to capture the player's interest.
*   **Branching Events:** The "Yuki (dog) event" is already implemented, featuring player choices that lead to different outcomes.
*   **UX Accessibility:** Current UI issues were noted, specifically white text blending into the map. Clients recommended a minimum **size 10 font** and using shadows or background colors to improve contrast.
*   **Location Accuracy:** Suggestions were made to update landmark photos (e.g., using the new Bruce Hall wing) and to use iconic starting points like **Uni Lodge**.

**Client Strategy for Demo**
*   **Selling the Vision:** The clients emphasized that the team should act like a **startup seeking funding**. The goal is to sell the "vision and the dream" rather than just listing technical features.
*   **Movie Trailer Approach:** The pitch should be compelling and focused, like a movie trailer, highlighting the most attractive elements to get "buy-in" from the audience.
