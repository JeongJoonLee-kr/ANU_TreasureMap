### Meeting Minutes

**Game Development & UX Feedback**
*   **New Features:** The team implemented 7 events, a treasure book, and a collection system. A "user profile" collection at the start was also added.
*   **UI Layout Change:** Clients suggested moving the top header bar to the bottom (interactive footer) to free up space for the map and improve font readability.
*   **Accessibility:** Font sizes should be increased from 4-6 to at least 10-12 for the game, and 16 for any presentation slides.
*   **Visual Clarity:** Toning down connection lines between nodes and adding background labels for text was recommended to improve map engagement.

**Critical Prioritization: Onboarding**
*   **The "First Impression":** Susan and Thomas strongly advised focusing on a tight **onboarding experience** rather than filling every single node with events. 
*   **The "Vertical Slice" Approach:** A complete experience (Onboarding → 3 high-quality nodes → Offboarding) is richer than a vast map with no guidance.
*   **Unfinished Content:** Unimplemented nodes should be grayed out or marked with "Coming Soon" to manage player expectations.

**Showcase Strategy**
*   **Infinite Resources:** For the demo, players should be given "infinite time" (e.g., 500 points) to allow full exploration of the available content.
*   **Self-Managed Demo:** The game must function without team intervention so that visitors can understand and play it even if the team is busy talking to others.

**Poster & Pitch Video Refinement**
*   **Poster Hierarchy:** The design should guide the reader's gaze sequentially (Goal → Activities → Outcomes). The **QR code (Call to Action)** must be in the bottom right.
*   **Branding & Credit:** Team names, roles, course codes, and the "College of Systems and Society" must be clearly visible for professional credit and future reusability.
*   **Pitch Style:** Avoid excessive "memes." The pitch should be professional, energetic, and act as a "startup funding request" that sells the vision.
*   **Process Evidence:** Include photos or videos of the team exploring the campus and mapping trajectories to show the project's research depth.

**Sound & Legal**
*   **Atmosphere:** Use adventure-themed music for the intro and peaceful/chilling tracks for offboarding or specific quiet locations.
*   **Compliance:** Ensure all music is royalty-free or properly credited to avoid copyright issues.
