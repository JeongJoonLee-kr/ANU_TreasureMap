**Final Deliverable Scope**
*   **Vertical Slice:** The team will deliver a "Playable Week 1 Slice" including the start page, onboarding, and the first week of gameplay.
*   **Concept Presentation:** Technical insights, the database schema, and ideas that couldn't be implemented will be captured in a **presentation deck** to demonstrate the project's depth.
*   **Future Research:** The presentation will serve as a research-style document (methods, findings, future work) to assist potential future teams.

**Refining Gameplay & UX**
*   **Simplified Resource:** The team confirmed they have simplified the complex energy/money system into a single resource: **"Time"**.
*   **Branching Path (Yuki Event):** Using the "Yuki (dog) event" as an example, players move between 13 map regions, making choices that lead to treasure collection or missed opportunities.
*   **UX Accessibility:** The clients noted that the current UI (e.g., white text on white background) lacks contrast. They emphasized a "glasses-off test"—if you can't find elements without glasses, the contrast is too low.

**Client Strategy for Demo**
*   **Scripted Tutorial:** To ensure a "Happy Path" experience for testers, the clients suggested **scripting or forcing choices** during the tutorial week so players don't miss the core treasure-hunting loop.
*   **Start from the Hive:** The team was advised to set the starting point in a familiar location, such as **The Hive (Computing)**, to ensure narrative accuracy.
