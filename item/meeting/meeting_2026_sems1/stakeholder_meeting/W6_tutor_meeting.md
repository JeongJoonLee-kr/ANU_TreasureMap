## Meeting Minutes
- The team walked through Sprint 5 progress, including paper prototyping, tutorial-first scope clarification, Jira action tracking, and current build progress across menu, UI, resource/status display, and tutorial flow.
- The tutor noted that documentation needed to become stronger and more traceable. In particular, the team should record what is going well, what is not going well, wins, blockers, and what should not be repeated in the next sprint.
- The tutor recommended that internal meetings use a clearer weekly stand-up structure so current work and blockers are visible rather than remaining ad hoc.
- The tutor also recommended that any team decision affecting scope, direction, or expectations should be sent to the client for confirmation rather than assumed internally.
- The tutor advised that sprint goals should be shared with the client at the start of the sprint so the team can receive confirmation or correction before the sprint drifts too far.
