## Meeting Minutes
- **Unified Game Concept Briefing**
  - **Core Objective**: To encourage students to explore unfamiliar parts of the ANU campus, such as the science or art buildings, through a gamified experience [8].
  - **Visual Style & Perspective**: A 2D top-down view inspired by games like Pokémon, Zelda, or Stardew Valley [9].
  - **Exploration Mechanics**: Features include "Fog of War" where the map brightens as the player moves, an exploration percentage tracker, and a starting point selection based on the player's residence [9, 10].
  - **The "Legacy" System**: Inspired by *NieR: Automata*, players can choose to sacrifice their progress or "treasures" to leave hints or help for future players, creating a collaborative hunt [11-13].
  - **Indoor Mini-games**: Two options were proposed for building interiors to manage the tight schedule: 
    - **Option 1 (Cludo-style)**: A guessing game involving rooms (e.g., Hive, Labs) and specific treasures [14, 15].
    - **Option 2 (Eldritch Horror-style)**: A turn-based system with map pins, adjacent movement, and random encounters/tasks to find clues [13, 16, 17].
  - **Collection**: Players maintain a "Diary" to collect found treasures or recorded notes [1].

- **Individual Roles and Interests**
  - The team discussed their professional development goals for the semester:
    - **Jeongjoon (June)**: Project Manager, focusing on coordination, hands-on management, and ensuring timely delivery [4, 18].
    - **UI/UX & Translation**: Focus on translating "treasures" into game assets and UI design [19].
    - **Development (Front/Back-end Bridge)**: Interest in combining back-end coding with front-end experiences (art/music) and improving team workflow communication [20, 21].
    - **Game Mechanics/Dungeon Master**: Leveraging experience in tabletop games to design playable characters, encounters, and core mechanics [22, 23].
    - **General Development**: Gaining foundational game development experience and software collaboration [24, 25].

- **Logistics, Risks, and Feedback**
  - **Tool**: **Godot** will be the primary engine due to its open-source nature and balance of coding/pre-made asset support [26].
  - **Risks**: High risks include the team's lack of prior game dev experience, the difficulty of creating unique ANU building assets from scratch (potential AI tool usage), and a very tight deadline [27].
  - **Playtesting**: Clients emphasized that user feedback should be viewed as an opportunity to improve the product rather than just extra work [28, 29].
