# Sprint 6 Checklist Coverage Matrix

This document translates the TechLauncher Sprint Checklist into item-by-item Sprint 6 closure evidence. It is written for review evidence, not as a game README. ## Closure Policy

- **Covered** means the item has visible evidence in Jira, GitHub, meeting notes, the running build screenshot, or Sprint 6 documentation.
- **Partially covered** means the item is planned and tracked, but final Week 9 or user-supplied proof is still needed.
- **Not claimed** means the team should not mark the item complete until evidence is supplied.
